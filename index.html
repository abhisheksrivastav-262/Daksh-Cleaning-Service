🧹 What's Included in Deep Cleaning?

We move methodically room-by-room to clean areas regular sweeps never reach.

🍳 Kitchen Deep Cleaning

  • • Chimney & exhaust fan external degreasing.
  • • Degreasing of kitchen wall tiles, slab, and stove.
  • • Scrubbing of sink, taps, and water fittings.
  • • Wiping cabinets inside-out (if empty, else outer wipe).
  • • Floor Mopping: A shiny finish for all flooring and tiled areas.

🧽 Bathroom Sanitization

  • • Acid descaling of toilet bowl, sink, and tiles.
  • • Removal of hard-water spots from taps and showerheads.
  • • Cleaning of mirrors, windows, and light fittings.
  • • Wall tile chemical wash and floor scrubbing.
  • • Final disinfecting spray treatment.

🛏 Living & Bedrooms

  • • Ceiling fan blades, tube lights, and AC exteriors dusted.
  • • Dry suctioning of sofas, mattresses, and carpets.
  • • Dusting and wiping of wardrobes and cabinets outer faces.
  • • Sliding window frame channels vacuumed and cleaned.
  • • Floor Mopping: A shiny finish for all flooring and tiled areas.

🌱 Balconies & Windows

  • • Balcony floor washing and railing scrub down.
  • • External glass facade wiping (where accessible).
  • • Dusting of main doors, gates, and safety grills.
  • • Removal of cobwebs from corners and ceiling.
  • • Eco-friendly waste disposal.
