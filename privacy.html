Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we handle and protect your data.

📋

Information We Collect

Personal Information: We collect your name, phone number, email address, and physical service address when you request a quote or book a cleaning service on our site.

Service Details: Information about your home size (BHK), service preferences, preferred appointment dates, and any special instructions or requirements you input.

Communication Records: Records of our chat transcripts, phone call logs, and messages exchanged via WhatsApp to facilitate customer care and service execution.

⚙️

How We Use Your Information

Service Delivery: To organize, execute, and dispatch our professional cleaning crew to your location.

Customer Support: To reply to quotes, coordinate scheduling, answer queries, and manage billing or feedback.

Quality Assurance: To review and enhance our cleaning standards and site experience based on user interactions and feedback reviews.

🔒

Data Security Measures

Secure Storage: All lead information submitted via our forms is encrypted and securely routed to WhatsApp/our coordinators. No plain text data is shared publicly.

Restricted Access: Only our operational managers and supervisor staff who require data to coordinate jobs are granted access to client addresses and contact logs.

Zero Data Sale: We strictly do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal addresses or phone numbers with third-party marketing companies.

🤝

Information Sharing

Service Crew: We share your service address and phone number with the assigned cleaning supervisor and crew members on the day of service for coordination.

Legal Requirements: We may share personal information if required by court orders or governmental regulations to comply with legal processes or protect security rights.
